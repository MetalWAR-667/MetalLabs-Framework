# Prompt Master

**Estado:** Active

---

# Objetivo

Este documento define el lenguaje visual utilizado para generar todas las ilustraciones del proyecto.

No describe escenas concretas.

Describe el estilo artístico permanente del juego.

Cada ilustración únicamente añadirá una breve descripción de la escena correspondiente.

La mayor parte del prompt permanecerá inalterada durante todo el proyecto.

---

# Filosofía

La IA no diseña la dirección artística.

La IA interpreta una dirección artística previamente definida.

El objetivo es conseguir que todas las ilustraciones parezcan realizadas por el mismo artista y pertenezcan al mismo universo visual.

---

# Prompt Maestro

## Estilo

Black and white nineteenth-century engraving.

Highly detailed ink illustration.

Inspired by classical fantasy engravings.

Crosshatching.

Fine line work.

Drypoint engraving.

Subtle charcoal textures.

High contrast.

Elegant composition.

No digital painting look.

No concept art style.

Timeless illustration.

---

## Atmósfera

Dreamlike.

Silent.

Ancient.

Melancholic.

Impossible.

Sacred.

Vast.

Lonely.

Mysterious.

The world feels mathematically ordered but emotionally incomprehensible.

## Arquitectura

Impossible architecture.

Monumental scale.

Massive gates.

Infinite stairs.

Unnatural perspectives.

Ancient ruins.

Structures obey unknown geometric laws.

Escher-inspired spatial logic.

No modern buildings.

---

## Espacio

Immense empty spaces.

Deep fog.

Invisible horizon.

Floating structures.

Bottomless voids.

Slow moving clouds.

Large negative space.

---

## Iluminación

Soft directional light.

Volumetric fog.

Subtle bloom.

Gentle contrast.

No hard sunlight.

No artificial lights.

Light should feel impossible rather than realistic.

---

## Composición

Vertical illustration.

Single focal point.

Strong silhouette.

Large readable shapes.

Designed to fit inside a card frame.

Leave comfortable margins around the main subject.

Avoid important elements near image borders.

Image Format

Landscape composition.
Aspect ratio 16:9.

Compose the scene as a wide cinematic illustration.

The subject should never fill the entire frame.

Reserve generous negative space to emphasize the scale of the architecture and the insignificance of the protagonist.

The composition should remain suitable for later cropping into different aspect ratios if necessary.


Keep the main focal point slightly off-center using cinematic composition principles.

Leave breathing space around the protagonist and the architecture.

Avoid placing important elements touching the image borders.

Image Format and Motion Safe Area

Always generate the illustration as a horizontal cinematic 16:9 composition.

The image will be displayed slightly enlarged and slowly zoomed out inside a cropped frame.

Keep all essential subjects and narrative focal points within the central 80% of the image.

Leave generous environmental detail and negative space around the edges to allow safe cropping, subtle camera movement and scaling.

Do not place essential faces, characters, structures or coloured focal points close to the borders.

## Dirección artística

Influences:

- Gustave Doré
- Arthur Rackham
- Lord Dunsany
- H. P. Lovecraft (without iconic imagery)
- M. C. Escher (architectural logic only)

---

## Geometría inquietante

The world follows a precise mathematical order that humans cannot fully understand.

Architecture should suggest impossible geometry without becoming abstract.

Use perspective inconsistencies, impossible staircases, oversized doors, unusual spatial relationships and subtle geometric repetition.

The world should feel logically impossible rather than chaotic.

---

## Color

Default:

Pure monochrome.

Color appears only when explicitly requested by the narrative.

When color is present it must be subtle and symbolic.

Never saturated.

Never dominant.

# Scene Prompt

Cada ilustración únicamente añadirá una descripción breve.

Ejemplo:

"A solitary stone platform suspended above an endless abyss. A gigantic impossible gate stands in the distance while silent clouds spiral slowly around the horizon."

Todo el resto del prompt procede de este documento.

---

# Regla de Oro

Nunca modificar el Prompt Maestro para resolver una ilustración concreta.

Si una imagen no funciona:

- revisar la descripción de la escena;
- regenerar;
- ajustar únicamente la escena.

El Prompt Maestro solo cambiará si se decide modificar la dirección artística completa del proyecto.
