AWAKE THE MONSTER — MASTER ART PROMPT v1.0
Artistic Direction

Create a highly detailed nineteenth-century engraved illustration inspired by:

Gustave Doré
Arthur Rackham
Victorian book illustration
Antique scientific manuscripts
M. C. Escher (architectural language only)
Lord Dunsany dreamlike fantasy

The image must resemble an illustration printed inside an antique forgotten book documenting impossible dreams.

Use:

monochrome ink engraving
extremely fine crosshatching
subtle sepia paper tones
elegant composition
dramatic lighting
impossible architecture
vast empty spaces
gigantic scale
silence
melancholy

No modern digital painting.

No comic style.

No anime.

No concept art.

Everything must look engraved.

World

The protagonist has awakened inside an impossible dream.

Reality obeys no human logic.

Gravity is uncertain.

Ancient structures float over endless voids.

There are no monsters visible.

Only impossible architecture.

The world itself is the monster.

Architecture should feel inspired by Escher without copying his works.

Floating temples.

Impossible towers.

Endless arches.

Platforms suspended over infinite emptiness.

Massive structures disappearing into clouds.

Everything should feel abandoned for thousands of years.

The Protagonist

The protagonist is not a fantasy hero.

He is a contemporary biker in his mid-thirties.

He has long dark hair, slightly messy, reaching his shoulders, reminiscent of classic 90s antiheroes such as the protagonist silhouette from Blackthorne (without copying the character directly).

He has:

sleeveless black leather vest
faded dark jeans
heavy biker boots
muscular tattooed arms
fingerless gloves
weathered appearance
relaxed, fearless posture
hands hanging naturally
no visible weapons
no backpack
no fantasy equipment

He should look like someone who spent the previous night drinking too much, riding motorcycles and making terrible decisions...

...and then woke up somewhere impossible.

His expression is calm.

Almost defiant.

As if he refuses to admit that reality has stopped making sense.

He never dominates the composition.

He is tiny compared to the impossible architecture surrounding him.

Pose Rules

La escopeta rara vez apunta.

Normalmente descansa sobre el hombro.

Nunca adopta poses heroicas.

Nunca parece entrenado militarmente.

Siempre transmite cierta chulería.

La seguridad desaparece únicamente cuando el sueño consigue romperla.

Composition

Large cinematic composition.

The protagonist stands on an isolated stone platform.

No bridges.

No stairs.

No obvious path.

Only an enormous abyss beneath him.

Far away floats a colossal impossible structure.

It cannot be reached.

The scene should visually communicate:

"There is nowhere to go except into the void."

The protagonist should naturally face the distant structure.

Color

The entire illustration remains monochrome engraved.

Only ONE element contains colour.

Use a deep Indigo Violet:

#1A1528

This colour represents the impossible.

It should appear only in a single focal point.

Examples:

inside a distant portal
behind a monumental doorway
inside an impossible celestial fracture
through an impossible window
beneath an ancient arch

Never illuminate the whole scene.

The Indigo is not light.

It is presence.
